## Reuse and proposed contracts

Customer identity: add one reusable Customer/account identity and contact model, optional explicit Bid/Job links, and retain current text/document snapshots. Reuse business-control/search patterns, not vendor identity tables (suppliers have a different role). Existing records stay unresolved until explicitly linked; no automatic name/email deduplication or wholesale backfill. Product must define organization vs person identity, contact cardinality, edit authority, merge/archive rules, access and which new documents capture snapshots. Estimate/proposal customer data remains historical, not live synchronization.

Collaboration: reuse JobUpdateMentionTextarea/tokenization, document preview and canonical-file upload patterns, with a Bid-authorized adapter. Do not transplant Job-specific RPCs or copy private Inbox conversations into Bid Updates. Add explicit update mention rows, update↔file relationship and notification metadata/deep links; server validates recipient activity and Bid visibility. Product must decide mention eligibility/visibility, edit/delete behavior, existing Bid-file reuse vs new upload, attachment cleanup/history and orphan handling.

Reminders: prefer extending My Work context with Bid, keeping reminders private unless explicitly shared. Reuse due-date editor, task lifecycle and account_notifications idempotent keys. Add advance-days settings, due-day delivery, timezone and dispatch/idempotency state plus Bid context validation. Product must confirm recipient(s), notification channel/time/timezone, X=0 handling, past due dates, date edits, completion/cancellation and inaccessible/deleted Bid behavior. In-app notification is the proposed first channel; email/push is not assumed. No service/admin content-read bypass.

Material authority: use explicit approved material↔catalog↔Inventory relationships, including catalog namespace and package/unit authority, rather than a second material list. An additive mapping model is a candidate, not an approved schema. Decide whether vendor catalog identifies product or sourcing offer before choosing canonical keys. Unit conversions need exact authoritative lot/package capacity; the Blend planning fallback is not Inventory evidence.

Availability: record on-hand separately from stock eligible for this Job. General stock plus this Job's canonical reservations may qualify; other Jobs' reservations, temporary labels and unresolved legacy earmarks must remain excluded or unresolved. Do not subtract reservations twice: current balance rows are already partitioned by reservation identity. Product must confirm eligibility and stale-stock thresholds. Missing matches/null quantities/units are unknown, not zero. Aggregate repeated requirement lines before shortage allocation to avoid claiming the same stock twice. No stock reservation/PO/status writes. Receipt code sets last_counted_at during intake, so that timestamp alone must not be labeled verified physical count.

Documents: initial availability should be an advisory live panel; preserve current issued PDFs and accepted Blend arithmetic. Filling document columns later requires captured source values/time, versioned snapshots and deterministic regeneration rules. Never consult live Inventory while rendering historical issued documents.
